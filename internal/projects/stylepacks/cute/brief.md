# Cute / pastel

Use a warm, playful pet-farm look. Prefer a soft cream surface with strawberry
pink, butter yellow, mint, and sky blue accents. Use rounded cards, friendly
illustrated icons, gentle shadows, and generous spacing. Keep text dark enough
for comfortable reading. Motion should feel buoyant and short: a small lift or
scale on hover and a soft pop on success. Avoid sharp corners, heavy borders,
hard neon, and aggressive shakes. A small fabric-like title ribbon, a soft
pressed bottom lip on primary buttons, and a gentle one-degree panel tilt are
welcome when they help the farm theme; use them sparingly.
