# Cyber / gacha

Use a high-contrast cyber-gacha banner look. Keep an ink-black base with
electric cyan, ultraviolet, and hot magenta accents. Establish a clear visual
hierarchy with crisp cards, clipped corners used sparingly, bright status
labels, and strong focal art. Motion may be energetic but must remain legible:
use short slides, eased reveals, and one intentional emphasis effect at a time.
Avoid unreadable glow, constant pulsing, random tilt, and low-contrast body
copy. Use clipped title ribbons, a deliberate asymmetric tilt, and a bright
pressed lip on high priority controls when the banner needs more energy. Keep
those effects attached to meaning and avoid applying them to every panel.
