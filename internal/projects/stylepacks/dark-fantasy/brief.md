# Dark fantasy

Use a dark, tactile character-sheet look. Build around charcoal and deep plum
surfaces with parchment text, tarnished gold accents, and restrained crimson for
danger. Prefer framed panels, subtle texture, engraved or serif display type,
and deliberate asymmetry. Motion should be quiet and weighty: fades, small
slides, and a rare glow for meaningful state changes. Avoid glossy gradients,
toy-like bounce, and bright white surfaces. Use a parchment ribbon or folded
tab for section titles, a restrained one-degree panel tilt, and a dark pressed
lip beneath important buttons. These are visual conventions of this pack, not
universal UI mechanics.
